# Skill: Onboarding — Get to Know Aswath

## When

Run once at first setup. Before anything else. This is how the AI learns who Aswath is.

## How

Start with a warm introduction, then ask questions in batches. Don't rush — this is foundation.

### Introduction

> "Hey, I'm going to be your personal AI companion. To work well together, I need to understand you — not just what you do, but how you think, what you care about, and what you've learned.
>
> This is a deep onboarding. I'll ask you questions across different areas of your life. Be as honest as you can — nothing leaves this file unless you want it to.
>
> We can do this in chunks if you get tired. Ready to start?"

### Phase 1: Basics (3-5 minutes)

Ask one at a time:

**Q: What's your name, and what do you do day-to-day?**
- What you do for work/studies
- How you spend your typical day

**Q: Where are you in life right now?**
- Age (optional), location
- Living situation, work situation
- What stage you're at

**Q: What brought you here?**
- Why did you want this AI companion
- What do you hope it helps you with
- Any frustration with previous approaches

### Phase 2: Values & Principles (5-10 minutes)

**Q: What do you care about most?**
- Core values (family, freedom, growth, impact, security, etc.)
- What you'd never compromise on

**Q: What are your core principles — the rules you try to live by?**
- Personal mottos or beliefs
- Things you've learned the hard way

**Q: What are you allergic to?**
- Things you strongly dislike
- Behaviors you want the AI to avoid

### Phase 3: Mind & Thinking (5-10 minutes)

**Q: How do you think best?**
- Through writing? Talking? Doing?
- Visual learner? Sequential?

**Q: How do you make decisions?**
- What process do you use?
- Do you overthink? Rush? Ask others?

**Q: When you're stuck on a problem, what do you do?**
- Your problem-solving approach
- How you break through blocks

### Phase 4: Career & Work (5 minutes)

**Q: What are you working on right now?**
- Current projects
- What you're building/learning

**Q: What are your work goals?**
- Short-term (3-6 months)
- Medium-term (1-2 years)
- Long-term (3-5 years)

**Q: What's challenging you right now in work?**
- Biggest obstacles
- What you're struggling with

### Phase 5: Money & Decisions (5-10 minutes)

**Q: What's your financial situation?**
- Income, savings, debts (as comfortable sharing)
- Your financial philosophy

**Q: What are your financial goals?**
- What are you saving for?
- Any big purchases planned?

**Q: What financial mistakes have you made?**
- Lessons learned
- What you'd do differently

### Phase 6: Relationships (3-5 minutes)

**Q: Who matters most to you?**
- Key people in your life
- How they influence your decisions

**Q: What do you want your relationships to look like a year from now?**
- Goals around people

### Phase 7: Health & Energy (3-5 minutes)

**Q: How's your health and energy?**
- Physical, mental, emotional state
- What affects them

**Q: What does your ideal day look like?**
- How you want to feel
- Routines that matter

### Phase 8: Growth & Learning (3-5 minutes)

**Q: What are you learning right now?**
- Books, skills, topics
- What you want to master

**Q: What are your biggest weaknesses?**
- Where do you struggle
- What you want to improve

**Q: What mistakes taught you the most?**
- Past failures that shaped you
- Lessons you're still integrating

### Phase 9: Future & Legacy (3-5 minutes)

**Q: Where do you want to be in 5 years?**
- Big picture vision
- What success looks like

**Q: What do you want people to say about you?**
- How you want to be remembered
- Your legacy

### Phase 10: Fun & Personality (2-3 minutes)

**Q: What do you do for fun?**
- Hobbies, interests
- What recharges you

**Q: Anything else I should know about you?**
- Quirks, unusual facts
- Anything you want me to understand

## After Onboarding

1. Create comprehensive profile pages in `wiki/profile/`
2. Update `user.md` with identity
3. Set initial goals in `wiki/profile/goals.md`
4. File financial rules to `wiki/profile/financial.md`
5. Log key mistakes to `wiki/profile/mistakes.md`
6. Update `soul.md` to match communication style
7. Write first interaction to `wiki/interactions/`
8. Update `wiki/log.md`: `## [YYYY-MM-DD] onboarding | completed`

## File Everything to Wiki

All the insights from onboarding go into `wiki/profile/`:
- `identity.md` — name, situation, background
- `values.md` — what matters, principles
- `mind.md` — how you think, decide, solve problems
- `career.md` — work, projects, goals
- `financial.md` — money situation, rules, goals
- `relationships.md` — key people, relationship goals
- `health.md` — health, energy, routines
- `strengths.md` — skills, strengths, what you're good at
- `mistakes.md` — failures, lessons
- `preferences.md` — how you communicate, what you need

## Progression

This isn't a one-time thing. Keep updating profile as you learn more about Aswath.
