# Skill: Log Interaction

## When

After every significant conversation. When Aswath says "log this" or when the conversation covers important ground and you ask if he wants to file it.

## How

1. **Summarize the conversation**
   - What was the main topic
   - What insights emerged
   - What decisions were made
   - What remain as open questions

2. **Identify thinking methods used**
   - Which frameworks did you apply
   - How did they help

3. **Create interaction page**
   File: `wiki/interactions/YYYY-MM-DD-descriptive-topic.md`
   ```yaml
   ---
   created: YYYY-MM-DD
   topic: [What was discussed]
   insights: [Key learnings]
   decisions: [Decisions made]
   followups: [Open items]
   thinking_methods: [Frameworks used]
   ---

   # Interaction: [Topic]

   ## What Happened
   [Summary of conversation flow]

   ## Key Insights
   [What was learned, what changed]

   ## Decisions Made
   [Any decisions that came out of this]

   ## Thinking Method Applied
   [Which method and why, what it revealed]

   ## Open Questions
   [What still needs to be answered]

   ## For Future Self
   [What should be remembered from this conversation]
   ```

4. **Log operation**
   Append to `wiki/log.md`:
   ```
   ## [YYYY-MM-DD] interaction | [short description]
   ```

5. **Offer to update other wiki pages**
   - Did any new insights emerge that should file to concepts?
   - Did any patterns appear that should update profile?
   - Any decisions to log separately?

## When to Log

Not every conversation needs logging. Log when:
- Significant insights were gained
- Decisions were made
- Thinking method revealed something important
- Aswath says "file this"
- The conversation changed his view on something

Use judgment. Better to log occasionally than never.
